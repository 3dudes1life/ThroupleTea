About this episode

Somehow… we made it to Episode 30 of Season 2 — which means this is officially our 40th episode of A Little Throuple Tea.

And honestly, we’re just as surprised as you are that we still have this much to talk about.

This week, we’re looking back at what’s changed since we first sat down behind the microphones, what we were nervous to talk about in the beginning, the running jokes that somehow became part of the show, what we’ve learned about each other after 40 episodes, and where we want to take this thing next.

Of course, because it’s us, that quickly turns into mosquito nets, Daniel “kneading,” crusty blankets, hot vampire brothers, blown-out holes, travel disasters and several conversations that probably explain why our show is marked explicit.

We also talk about some pretty huge milestones for A Little Throuple Tea — including our new partnership with the Los Angeles Blade and the launch of the official Throuple Tea app on BOTH Apple and Android!

Plus, for the first time on the podcast, we ditch the physical slips of paper and play Chaos Bowl directly from the new app, using Classic Mode on Wild with Slay Cards. Things get weird immediately.
