About this episode

What exactly belongs on the ultimate Gay Bucket List?This week on A Little Throuple Tea, we’re going through the gay experiences, destinations, milestones, events, and ridiculous adventures the internet says every gay should experience at least once.We’re talking our favorite Pride memories, the cities we still want to experience Pride in, Fire Island, Palm Springs, Key West, Puerto Vallarta, gay cruises, circuit parties, drag brunches, gay ski weeks, concerts, chosen family, holding hands in public, supporting LGBTQ+ businesses, and finding places where you can actually feel completely comfortable being yourself.Of course, because it’s us, that somehow turns into Daniel needing to plan something for once, disappearing on vacation, straight boys, bathhouses, robot fantasies, gay milestones, planning a giant house party and a conversation about whether straight men actually wash their buttholes.Yes. It goes very far off the rails.We also get into the more meaningful side of the Gay Bucket List — finding your chosen family, experiencing Pride, falling in love, supporting your community and finding that sense of belonging that so many LGBTQ+ people spend years looking for.Plus, we play Chaos Bowl directly from the Throuple Tea app again, this time with Most Likely To prompts, Mature Mode, Wild Mode and Slay Cards. And apparently the app knows us a little too well.Also in this episode:- The ultimate Gay Bucket List- Our favorite Pride memories and dream Pride destinations- Gay experiences we’ve somehow never had- Chaos Bowl: Most Likely To + Wild + Slay Cards- Two real Throuple Hotline questions- A completely unnecessary investigation into straight men's hygiene- Sassy Astrology for August 21–27- Daniel’s Dad Joke- And several conversations that absolutely were NOT on the Gay Bucket ListIf you’ve already crossed half of these off your list, congratulations.
