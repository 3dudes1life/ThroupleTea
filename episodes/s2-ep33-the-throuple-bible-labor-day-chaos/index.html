About this episode

What happens when your new book is so massive it can’t even become a hardcover?

This week on A Little Throuple Tea, we’re celebrating Labor Day, revealing how Fault Lines became the 730-page “Throuple Bible” and explaining why formatting the book nearly broke us—or inspired us to build something completely new.

We also prepare for Southern California rain like a hurricane is coming, revisit one of the worst rescue plans in history and let Bowl of Chaos expose which one of us becomes dangerously confident after watching a single YouTube tutorial.

Then, the Throuple Hotline brings us a relationship problem involving a three-year boyfriend who is still being introduced to the family as “the friend.” How long do you keep the peace before it becomes disrespect?

Plus, Sassy Astrology, an emotional family tribute and one host ending the entire Labor Day episode with the wrong holiday.
