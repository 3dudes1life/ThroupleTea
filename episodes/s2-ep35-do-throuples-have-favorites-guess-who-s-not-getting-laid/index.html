About this episode

Do throuples have favorites—or just different partners for different parts of life?

This week on A Little Throuple Tea, we’re answering the question nobody in a throuple should probably discuss with microphones recording.

We compare the unique relationships between each pairing, who is easier to talk to, who gets things done, who needs the most reassurance and who apparently will not be getting laid this weekend.

Then Bowl of Chaos makes the conversation even messier with questions about jealousy, quality time, texting habits, keeping score and the fastest ways to piss each other off.

The Throuple Hotline also asks whether the three of us would ever create a spicy page. It sounds like a possible business opportunity—until worn underwear enters the planning process and the entire idea begins falling apart.

Plus, Sassy Astrology leads to a real conversation about careers, AI and knowing when it is time to stop forcing an old path and build something new.
