About this episode

What happens when three guys sit down to talk about podcasting and immediately lose control of the conversation?This week on A Little Throuple Tea, we somehow go from the episodes we actually remember to whether we interrupt each other more now, the ridiculous amount of work that happens after we stop recording, and why apparently none of us are actually listening when the other two are talking.Then things get considerably less productive.We invent an entirely new species called the pig frog, discover that our former “meat boy” may have become a flower boy, debate whether homemade cookies can be smuggled onto a cruise ship, and let Fireball make several decisions for us.We also get into something a little creepier: some of the haunting moments from Tres Amigos, Una Vida: Fault Lines were inspired by things that actually happened in our house—including a bedroom door mysteriously being wide open in the middle of the night.Then the Bowl of Chaos exposes who derails the show, who interrupts everybody, who tells stories the other two definitely did not approve for public consumption, and the uncomfortable realization that all three of us might just be waiting for our turn to talk.The Throuple Hotline asks where we actually record the podcast, which leads to the glamorous reveal that our “basement studio” is really a converted garage approximately one step below the rest of the house.Plus, Sassy Astrology comes for anyone overthinking their life, Zoe becomes our unexpected first podcast guest, and Daniel somehow finds an audiobook dad joke to close everything out.Also in this episode:• The Halloween episode none of us can forget• Why making one podcast episode can turn into three days of work• The return of completely unnecessary throuple arguments• Pig frogs and the science nobody requested• Our plans to eventually take the podcast on the road• The Bowl of Chaos + Fireball• Caleb from Nebraska calls the Throuple Hotline• Zoe crashes Sassy Astrology• review and share the show with somebody who would absolutely help us breed a pig frog.Throuple life. It doesn’t suck.
