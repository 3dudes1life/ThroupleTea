About this episode

What completely unimportant opinion would you defend like your life depended on it?This week on A Little Throuple Tea, we’re rapid-firing the pettiest hills we’ll die on, and somehow things get personal almost immediately.Pineapple on pizza? Ketchup on eggs? Ranch on pizza? Cold leftovers? Top sheets? Decorative pillows? Airport drinking before noon?Nothing is safe.Then Caleb admits to eating heated Miracle Whip sandwiches, which may be one of the most disturbing things we’ve learned about him in 38 episodes.We also get into the social rules people somehow still don’t understand: return your cart, move if you’re walking slow, let people off the elevator first, stop watching videos in public without headphones, and for the love of God, have your ID ready when you get to TSA.Then we tackle travel rules, birthday week, Christmas decorations, restaurant bills, Irish goodbyes, unexpected FaceTimes, six-text-message novels and whether the middle seat really deserves both armrests.The Petty as F*** Bowl of Chaos makes everything even worse as we expose who blocks people first, who remembers something you said six years ago, who loves being proven right, who screenshots messages immediately, and who absolutely knows how to get what they want from the other two.Plus, Sassy Astrology comes
