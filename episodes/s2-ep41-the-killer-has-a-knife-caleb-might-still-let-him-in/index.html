About this episode

Happy Halloween from A Little Throuple Tea. We showed up dressed as salt, lime and tequila and asked the only question that really matters: which one of us dies first in a horror movie?This week we throw our throuple into a slasher movie, a haunted house and a full zombie apocalypse to see who actually survives.Caleb is apparently willing to go check the noise, Daniel thinks he can Reiki the killer, Will already has the baseball bat, and somehow a man showing up with a knife turns into a debate over whether the bulge makes the risk worth it.Then we use actual evidence.When Caleb thought a bear was coming after us in Alaska, he ran first and asked questions later. So naturally, his horror-movie survival score is already questionable.From there, we move into haunted-house territory. Would we spend the night in one for $25,000? Who investigates the noise? Who talks to the ghost? Who runs first? And after admitting we already messed with a Ouija board years ago, the answer to whether we would do that again is a very firm NO.We also get into the creepy stuff we can’t totally explain, including shadows in mirrors, lights acting weird and those moments when a place just gives you the feeling that you need to leave.Then civilization ends.In our zombie apocalypse, Daniel still wants to make tri-tip, somebody is apparently checking the mail, Starbucks is somehow still on the itinerary, and the three of us quickly learn that teamwork may not be enough to save everyone.Eventually we lock in the official horror-movie death order. Feelings are hurt.
