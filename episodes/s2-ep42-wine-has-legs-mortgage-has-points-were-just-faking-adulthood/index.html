About this episode

Absolutely — here it is cleaned up for easy copy/paste with proper spacing and no weird source text stuck in the middle:Adulting is basically saying “yeah, totally” and then Googling it later.This week on A Little Throuple Tea, we’re admitting the stuff we pretend to understand even when we absolutely do not.Mortgage points? Wine legs? Confit? Amuse-bouche? Torque? Gen Z slang? Sports rules? Corporate buzzwords?We put ourselves on the spot and find out which things we actually know, which things we kind of know, and which things we have apparently been confidently faking for years.It starts with adulting and money, where “mortgage points” somehow becomes: point one, you have a mortgage. Point two, pay on time.Then we move into wine and food vocabulary and discover that none of us knows why wine has “legs,” chiffonade sounds like an insult, and amuse-bouche apparently sounds more like a booze cruise.We also test our knowledge of sports, cars, house repairs, Gen Z language and business jargon.Rizz? Delulu? Torque versus horsepower? Golf scoring?Somehow we know more about KPIs, deliverables, bandwidth and pivoting than we do about half the things adults are supposedly expected to know.And yes, Daniel handles a spam call in Spanish just well enough to make us wonder if he is secretly a spy.We also got invited to screen One Baby, Please on OUTtv, which somehow fits perfectly into an episode where we immediately admit we know absolutely nothing about babies.Including the time Daniel put a diaper on backwards.Then Bowl of Chaos turns into the Caleb Show as we find out who accepts dares they absolutely should not, who disappears from the group for 20 minutes with no explanation, who tells strangers way too much personal information, and who would impulsively book a trip for tomorrow.Also in this episode:• Daniel
