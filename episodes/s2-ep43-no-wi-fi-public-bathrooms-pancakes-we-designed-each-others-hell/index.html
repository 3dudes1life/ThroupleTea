About this episode

How well do you really know your partners? Enough to build their perfect personal hell?

This week on A Little Throuple Tea, we take everything we’ve learned about each other over the years — the pet peeves, food issues, routines, social limits and tiny things that drive us absolutely insane — and use all of it against each other.

The mission: design one full day from hell for each of us.

Wake-up time. Breakfast. Outfit. Transportation. Job. Lunch. Dinner. Evening plans. Bedtime.

And apparently we know each other a little too well.

Daniel’s nightmare involves early mornings, food he hates, way too many people and an outfit nobody should be forced to wear in public.

Caleb discovers that his true personal hell may simply be boredom, isolation, no internet and absolutely nothing productive to do.

And Will learns that public bathrooms, customer service, traffic and wasting time may be enough to completely break him.
