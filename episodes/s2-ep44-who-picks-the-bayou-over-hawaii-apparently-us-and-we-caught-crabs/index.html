About this episode

We were supposed to be in Hawaii.Instead, we loaded two beagles into the Tundra and drove 27 hours east.This week on A Little Throuple Tea, we’re finally catching up on the last few weeks of chaos — including the family emergency that changed our Hawaii plans at the last minute and somehow turned our vacation into a road trip to Louisiana.What started as Plan B quickly became Las Cruces, Houston, our first Buc-ee’s, a ferry through Galveston, a house on stilts in the bayou, fishing off the dock, swamp tours, alligators, blue crabs and our first trip to New Orleans.And naturally, none of it went completely according to plan.There’s a closed freeway, a questionable escape up an off-ramp, a serious argument about whether our fish had worms, a catfish incident that made one of us immediately reconsider fishing, and at least one unexplained moment at our Airbnb after spending the night hearing ghost and vampire stories in New Orleans.Then we come home and realize Thanksgiving is basically already here.So we get into the food, the traditions, the Thanksgiving disasters we still remember, Friendsgiving versus family, and the questions relatives really need to stop asking at the dinner table.Also in this episode:• Bowl of Chaos starts the show with cursed superpowers • Why our Hawaii trip had to be canceled • Driving across the country with Rigsby and Zoey • Our first Buc-ee’s experience • Fishing and catching blue crabs in the Louisiana bayou • The redfish worm argument that would not die • A very angry catfish • Alligators basically everywhere • Ghosts, vampires and weird New Orleans history • The Thanksgiving turkey disaster that ended with dinner at midnight • Questions nobody wants from relatives • Sassy Astrology becomes a Thanksgiving survival guide • Daniel Slips the Tip • Another Dad Joke nobody requested This week’s Sassy Astrology also gives us a few reminders for the holiday:Not every incorrect statement at the dinner table requires your rebuttal.Sometimes the tea can stay in the pot.And most importantly:Eat the pie, mind your business, and take a nap.Tell us: what Thanksgiving question are YOU sick of answering?
